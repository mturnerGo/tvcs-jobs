TVCS Enrollment Qualification Criteria

The Villages Charter School operates in partnership with The Villages under Florida Statute § 1002.33. Enrollment admission is granted based on parent employment priority tiers:

Category Eligible Employers Admission Priority
Category I Direct employees of The Villages of Lake-Sumter, Inc., Holding Company, TVCS teachers & staff, Properties of The Villages, Recreation. 1st Priority Lottery
Category II Direct full-time operational subcontractors (e.g. Jacobs Engineering / utilities, Sumter County BOCC). 2nd Priority Lottery
Category III Full-time employees of commercial tenants leasing physical business space from The Villages across town squares and commercial plazas. 3rd Priority Lottery
Category IV Full-time employees of approved vendors and regional business partners. 4th Priority Tier
Mandatory Full-Time Requirement: Florida statutory guidelines require continuous full-time employment (typically 30–40 hours/week) during the student's enrollment. Part-time positions do not meet school eligibility requirements.
